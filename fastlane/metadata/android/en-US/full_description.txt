Attablés is a 100% offline, account-free personal dinner and invitation journal designed to help you host friends and family thoughtfully without culinary missteps.

PLAN GATHERINGS AT A GLANCE
• Track Planned and Realised meals, whether hosted at your home or someone else's.
• Group guests by household to select an entire family in one tap.
• Look back anytime at who attended and what dishes were served.

NEVER MISS AN ALLERGY
• Each person has their own list: tap "+ Add" on their card, pick an allergen (peanuts, shellfish, gluten...) or type any other food, then choose its level.
• Two levels: "Allergy" raises a strong red alert; "Avoids" (intolerance, diet, dislike) is shown for information.
• Your guests' allergies stay pinned right above the menu editor, in one simple form: "Peanuts — Léa".
• In the Dishes tab, note each dish's allergens, or tick "None of these allergens" when it contains none: a dish containing a guest's allergen is flagged at once.
• If a guest has an allergy and a dish on the menu has no allergens noted yet, a reminder appears under it: "Allergens for this dish not filled in — check". One tap lets you fill them in. "None of these allergens" only covers the listed allergens: a food typed by hand for a guest (kiwi...) still needs checking.

ANTI-DUPLICATE DISH MEMORY & RATINGS
• Stop accidentally serving the same dish twice to the same guests.
• Configurable time window (6 months by default): "Recently served" reminder for recent dishes, gentle "can return" hint for older ones.
• Rate realised meals and individual dishes (Favorite, Good, Avoid) to keep winners and skip misses.

OPTIONAL ANONYMIZED MARKDOWN EXPORT
• Copy a structured Markdown summary of constraints and dish history in one tap.
• Anonymized by default: first names and the host name are left out; your free-text notes are copied as typed, so reread them before pasting.

100% LOCAL & OPEN SOURCE
• Works completely offline with zero ads, zero trackers, and zero telemetry.
• Local backup and restore via standard .json files.
