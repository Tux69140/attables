Attablés is a 100% offline, account-free personal dinner and invitation journal designed to help you host friends and family thoughtfully without culinary missteps.

PLAN GATHERINGS AT A GLANCE
• Track Planned and Realised meals, whether hosted at your home or someone else's.
• Group guests by household to select an entire family in one tap.
• Look back anytime at who attended and what dishes were served.

NEVER MISS AN ALLERGY
• Clearly separate strict allergies (peanuts, shellfish, gluten...) from mild preferences or diets.
• View all combined dietary constraints for your current guest list in real time.
• Keep a permanent strict-allergy reminder banner right above the menu editor.

ANTI-DUPLICATE DISH MEMORY & RATINGS
• Stop accidentally serving the same dish twice to the same guests.
• Configurable time window (6 months by default): strong alert for recently served dishes, gentle "can return" hint for older ones.
• Rate realised meals and individual dishes (Favorite, Good, Avoid) to keep winners and skip misses.

OPTIONAL ANONYMIZED MARKDOWN EXPORT
• Copy a structured Markdown summary of constraints and dish history in one tap.
• Anonymized by default: no guest or host names are included, only headcount, dietary restrictions, and dish history.

100% LOCAL & OPEN SOURCE
• Works completely offline with zero ads, zero trackers, and zero telemetry.
• Local backup and restore via standard .json files.
