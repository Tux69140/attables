Version 0.3.0:
• Guided welcome, new saffron palette (light and dark) and new icons.
• Each guest has their own sheet of what they don’t eat: allergies and foods they avoid.
• Allergies stay visible in the invitation; a risky dish, or one whose allergens aren’t filled in, is flagged.
• New “Plats” (Dishes) tab: check an idea, note dishes, look back at what was served.
• Back saves, “Annuler” (Undo) recovers; better screen reader and large text support.
